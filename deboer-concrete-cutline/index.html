Field notes / general questions

The finish is only one line on the ticket.

These are general questions a buyer can ask any concrete contractor. They are not a statement of DeBoer’s materials, specifications, or process.

01

What is under it?

Ask how the base will be prepared, compacted, and graded before concrete arrives.

02

How thick is the pour?

Make the planned thickness explicit for the load the slab is expected to carry.

03

What reinforcement is specified?

Ask whether the plan calls for rebar, mesh, another system, or no reinforcement.

04

Where are the joints?

Ask how the joint layout relates to the slab geometry and likely movement.

05

What finish fits the use?

Foot traffic, vehicle traffic, weather, and appearance all affect that choice.

06

What happens on pour day?

Ask who is on site, how timing is handled, and what conditions would change the plan.
