# Envious Landscape — Fenton, MI

**Status:** read on record, not built
**Their site:** enviouslandscape.com
**Score:** 29 plumbing + 43 design = **72** (both re-measured today, identical)
**Read written:** 27 August 2026

## The read

The best raw material on this board, and the least of it on the first screen.

**Ten Google reviews, full text, names, dates, all readable in the DOM.** Not
widget-locked like Goblin's. The Trustindex plugin renders the whole thing as
plain markup, and the owner has replied to every one of them. They are long. Gina
Cossin wrote five paragraphs. Andrew Bates, a disabled veteran, wrote roughly four
hundred words in July 2019 about a new-build lot that was "just sand and rocks."
Darryl Viv: *"OK its been 6 years since we started using Envious."*

Read them together and one thing keeps happening. **They name the staff.**

- Cindy Wulbert: *"Jamie, the designer was knowledgeable..."*
- Mary Berry: *"I know if there is ever an issue or future needs I can contact Jamie to take care of us."*
- Mike Majerowicz: *"Jamie came out quickly... Mike, Don and Devin did a great job with the install."*
- Gina Cossin: *"Jamie made the design process seamless. She listened to my ideas..."*
- Ben Schoener: *"a great experience working with Don."*
- Andrew Bates names **Charlie**, **Stacey** in the office, **Cody** the sprinkler
  man of 30+ years, plus Jason, Tim, Lee and Paul.

**Not one of those people exists anywhere on the website.** No About page bio, no
team section, no photograph, no name. Grep the About page, the homepage and the
contact page for Jamie, Charlie or Stacey and every hit is inside a customer's
review or the owner's reply to one. Customers are asking for a designer by name,
and the site has never heard of her.

The photography situation is a separate finding, and a worrying one. The Work
Gallery holds **29 items**, and every single filename is shaped like this:

```
imgi_11_5cd241_7fd9b0b2c8aa433e9138b6254c56de95mv2.webp
imgi_54_5cd241_515d750f44ca453d90dd4522e075adecmv2.webp
```

`5cd241_<32 hex>~mv2` is Wix's media naming convention. `imgi_NN_` is the prefix an
image-downloader tool adds. All 29 were uploaded to `/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/`,
the same month the WordPress site was built. **The portfolio was scraped off their
own old Wix site rather than re-uploaded from originals.** Measured four of them:
656x492, 967x544, 369x492, 286x170. Web derivatives, not photographs.

Meanwhile the homepage has **eleven `<img>` tags and not one is a finished job**:
two logos, seven 50x50 reviewer avatars, one 208x241 award ribbon
(`imgi_18_2024-BEST-Ribbon.webp`, `alt=""`, no caption, nothing anywhere saying
what the award is), and one 286x170 Wix leftover. The four service tiles are CSS
background images. 59% of the first screen is spent before the headline arrives,
and the headline, "BE THE ENVY" at 140px, is the one genuinely confident piece of
design on the site. There is **no form on the homepage at all**.

Real credentials, stated plainly on their own About page: **since 1998**, the
Installation Foreman is **ICPI certified and a licensed builder**, warranties
offered, HomeAdvisor screened, financing through HFS Financial, Genesee, Oakland
and Livingston counties. Counters claim **27 years in business**, **500,000+ sq ft
of landscaping mapped**, **2,500+ homeowners served**. A six-step process ending in
**U-Vision 3-D plans**.

**Angle:** five customers named your designer in their reviews. Your website has never mentioned her once.

## Treatment

| Decision | Call |
|---|---|
| Photography | **29 jobs, all scraped.** Usable at mid-size, nothing survives a full-bleed hero. Ask Charlie or Jamie for camera originals before building. If they cannot produce them, build a mosaic gallery at honest size and put the crew and the reviews on the first screen instead. Do not upscale a scrape. |
| Prices | None published anywhere. None on the page. HFS Financial is linked in the nav, so financing is real and can be named, but no rate or term is stated and none should be invented. |
| Reviews | **The strongest set on the board, and fully readable.** Quote verbatim with names and dates. Ten of them. Use the ones that name staff, because the staff is the angle. |
| Credentials | 1998, ICPI-certified and licensed-builder foreman, HomeAdvisor screened, warranties offered. All stated on their own About page, so state them exactly. The **2024 BEST ribbon has no label on their own site**, so do not say what it is for. The counters (27 years, 500,000+ sq ft, 2,500+ homeowners) are their claims, so attribute them as their claims. |
| Structure | People first. A crew section with real names is the build. Then the work, then the reviews that name the crew, so the two halves prove each other. Add a form, because there is not one. |

**One correction to carry into outreach:** the board lists them as Grand Blanc.
Their own site says **Fenton, Michigan**, on every page. Use Fenton.

**The photo question decides the build, so ask it first.** Everything else here is
unusually strong, but a landscaper's page that cannot show a patio at full width is
a different page. Find out whether the originals exist before promising a gallery.
