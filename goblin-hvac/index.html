Straight answers

Questions people actually ask.

How do I know if my system needs service?

If it isn’t holding a consistent temperature, it’s making a noise it didn’t used to make, or your energy bill jumped without the weather explaining it, something is off. Short-cycling — kicking on and off every few minutes — is worth a call on its own.

Catching it early is usually the difference between a part and a system.

Do you really answer at night and on weekends?

Yes — we’re open 24 hours, seven days a week, so you can reach us and get the job logged whatever time the system decides to quit.

Urgent heating and cooling problems are prioritised for the soonest available visit. If it can safely wait until morning we’ll tell you that rather than push a call-out you don’t need.

Should I repair it or replace it?

Depends on age, the specific failure and what the repair costs against a new system. A fifteen-year-old furnace with a cracked heat exchanger is a replacement. A ten-year-old unit needing a capacitor is a repair, and anyone telling you otherwise is selling.

We’ll give you the honest read and the numbers for both, then you decide.

What brands do you work on?

Air conditioners, furnaces and water heaters across all major brands. We install Goodman, Amana, Daikin, Navien and Fujitsu, and we service whatever is already in your house regardless of who put it there.

What does a service call cost?

We give you upfront pricing before any work begins — you approve the number first. What we won’t do is quote a repair over the phone from a description of a noise, because that number would be a guess and guesses are how people end up paying for parts they didn’t need.

Maintenance plan members get 10–20% off repairs depending on tier.

Are the maintenance plans worth it?

If your system is more than about five years old, generally yes. Two inspections a year catch the failures that turn into no-heat calls, and priority scheduling matters a great deal on the first properly cold night of the year when everyone is calling at once.

The Warrior plan pays for itself on one moderate repair. If your system is nearly new, Hatchling is plenty.

What payment do you take?

Cash, check and card.
