Privacy

What we do with your details.

Short version: we use them to quote your job and nothing else. We do not sell them, and there is no advertising or tracking on this site.

What this site collects

If you use the estimate form, you choose what to tell us. That is the job you want poured, roughly how big it is, the town it is in, when you are hoping to have it done, a site visit time that suits you, and your name and phone number.

Nothing is collected until you type it. Browsing the site collects nothing, because there are no analytics, no advertising pixels and no third-party scripts on the page.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own and uses no tracking cookies.

What we do with it

We use your details to work out a number for your job, arrange a visit to look at it, and contact you about that job. We will not add you to a mailing list.

Who else sees it

Your estimate reaches us either as an email you send from your own email app, or through a form-delivery service that passes it to our inbox. That service handles the message in transit and does not use it for anything else. Our email is hosted by Google Workspace. Nobody else is given your details, and we do not sell or trade them.

How long we keep it

Enquiries stay in our email and our job records for as long as we need them for the work and for our business records.

Asking us to delete it

Email nick@deboerconcreteandconstructionllc.com or call (248) 212-9577 and ask, and we will delete what we hold, apart from anything we have to keep for tax or legal reasons.

Children

This site is for people buying concrete work and is not aimed at children.

Changes

If this changes, the new version goes on this page.
