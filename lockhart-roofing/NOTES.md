## The read

Family owned since **1982**. Forty-four years, sixteen counties across the Lower
Peninsula, a real address at 2290 E Hill Rd #102 in Grand Blanc, and **(877) 766-3033**.

They spend money on being trusted, in three separate places:
**Birdeye** review software carrying a 5.0 from 19 reviews, **BBB accreditation at
A+ for over a decade**, and **Grand Blanc Chamber** membership. Three paid trust
signals, maintained deliberately, by a business that clearly cares how it is seen.

Every one of them currently points at a **suspended hosting account**.

Every URL on the domain redirects to `/cgi-sys/suspendedpage.cgi`. Not just the
homepage: `/about-us/`, `/services/`, `/grand-blanc-mi/` and
`/community-support/roofer-in-grand-blanc-michigan/` were all real, ranking pages,
and Google is now crawling suspension notices where local landing pages used to be.
It was alive as recently as **7 June**, so this is roughly eleven weeks old. Hosted
on GoDaddy shared hosting, which reads as an unpaid invoice rather than a hack.

That `/community-support/` path is worth noticing. A forty-four-year family roofer
with a community section is exactly the business whose reputation is entirely local
and entirely offline right now.

**Angle:** you are paying three companies to send people to a website that does not exist.
