# Buck & Bossman Construction — Genesee County, MI

**Status:** read on record, not built
**Their site:** buckandbossmanconstruction.com
**Score:** 37 plumbing + 39 design = **76**
**Read written:** 27 August 2026

## The read

Forty years of post-frame building in Michigan, and **the website is not theirs.**

It is a **Walters Buildings dealer template**. The logo in the header is Walters
Buildings. The hero says *"Build with Confidence …Build with Walters Buildings."*
The phone number is an **800 number**. The header badges read "Award Winning
Builder" and "The Exclusive Walters Buildings Dealer in Michigan", plus NFBA
membership.

Buck & Bossman's own name appears for the first time in the **third paragraph of
body copy**, below the fold.

Underneath the template there is real substance. The CSS backgrounds are
descriptively named photographs of actual Michigan buildings
(`pole-barn-metal-steel-michigan.jpg`, `shop-building-large-machine-storage-mi.jpg`,
`metal-building-for-storage-prefab-mi.jpg`), the category images cover residential,
farm, horse barns, commercial and shop, and there is a "Complete Picture Gallery".
Everything is served at **423px** inside a 2005 layout on `.php` URLs, with no meta
description, no Open Graph and no structured data.

**Angle:** this is Walters' website with your phone number on it. Forty years of your buildings, and your name is in paragraph three.

## Treatment

| Decision | Call |
|---|---|
| Photography | **They have it.** Real buildings, honestly named, just served small and buried. Pull the full-resolution originals from the gallery. This is a Viper-style photo-led build. |
| Prices | None published. None on the page. |
| Reviews | None found on the site. Check Google Business Profile before deciding. |
| Credentials | Walters dealer status and NFBA membership are real and worth keeping. **The distinction to draw: they are a Walters dealer, they are not Walters.** |
| Structure | Their name in the logo position. Their buildings at full size. The manufacturer relationship as a credential, not the brand. |

The sensitivity: they may be **contractually required** to run Walters branding.
Do not open by telling them their marketing is wrong. Open by showing what their
own name looks like at the top of a page, and let them raise the constraint.
