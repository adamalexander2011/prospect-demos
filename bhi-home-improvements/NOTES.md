## The read

Measured one gallery thumbnail and then its full-size original:

```
images/MI_Roofing_Photos/MI_Roofing_Builders_Fenton14-t.jpg    100 x 49    3.4 KB
images/MI_Roofing_Photos/MI_Roofing_Builders_Fenton14.jpg     1132 x 553  696 KB
```

**One hundred pixels wide.** The Photo Gallery holds **29 of them**, laid out in
nested `<table border="0">` elements, every one with `alt=""`, `title=""` and an
empty `<span class="ThumbCaption"></span>`. Clicking a thumbnail reloads the page
at `index.php?page=Photo_Gallery&p=0&c=All&l=14`. Another original measures
960x720. A slideshow file measures **1920x745 at 1.7MB**. The photography is real,
it is theirs, and the visitor is shown one twelfth of it.

The homepage has **zero heading elements**. No `<h1>`, no `<h2>`, nothing. It opens
with a manufactured FAQ:

> *Q: "Where can I find the best Lower Michigan roofing specialists?"*

Now open the About Us page, which is where the actual business is:

> *"The **Randall family history spans four generations with approximately 100
> years** serving the Lower Michigan area. BHI Home Improvements is lead by the
> owners and brothers **David and Jack** who take their renovation services to the
> next level. They learned these high quality trades and skills from their
> forefathers. **Their great grandfather, Olin, began this business back in the
> 1920's.**"*

A named great-grandfather. Four generations. A hundred years. Two brothers who have
**"been working together for 20 plus years."** And a slogan their own page signs:
**"The Smartest Way to do Your Homework," David Randall.**

None of that is on the homepage. The homepage says "carried on the traditions that
our grandfather began in the early 1920's" and stops.

Also on the About page, a **Featured Project** with before-and-after photography
described in the copy: a 2018 exterior renovation, *"a portico, curved barrel
covered porch entrance with columns, siding, new doors, and windows."* Before-and-
afters exist. They are on page two, under a heading that is not a heading.

**Ten Google reviews, quoted verbatim with full names**, across two pages. They are
unusually concrete:

- **Melvin Bell:** *"House, garage and shed were finished in only one day. 5 men on
  the job and when they left, my property was spotless."*
- **Andrew Weise:** *"I called two other companies, one didn't answer and the
  other's earliest opening was a week out. I called Dave with BHI and he was out in
  less than a half hour. Made the repairs quickly and in the pouring rain no less!"*
- **Phife Eighteen:** *"Had 9 different quotes. Chose BHI based upon the
  conversation I had with the owner."*
- **Dianne Russell** names the installer: *"Robert did an awesome install job... He
  even took the old door and trim with him."*
- Plus Andrew Bowling, Debbie Saville, Michael Marr, Rob G, Kevin Simpson.

Michael Marr's review is printed **twice on the homepage**, once with straight
quotes and once with curly ones.

Other published claims worth keeping: an **emergency line 24 hours a day**;
**"We promise a start date and deliver on or before our finish date"**; guaranteed
financing; fully licensed and insured; 13 named towns each with their own page.

And the most disarming sentence on this entire board, in their own list of services:

> **"Please note: we do not provide bathroom and kitchen services, nor snow plowing."**

Nobody writes that unless they mean it.

Address **14165 Fenton Rd #104B, Fenton, MI 48430**, phone (810) 423-5813, email
**bhihomes@aol.com**. Site by superiorweb.net, copyright 2026, no Open Graph, no
schema, no form anywhere, and the font stack in the CSS includes a typo: **`Airal`**.

**Angle:** Olin started this in the 1920s and four generations later the photographs of what your family built are 100 pixels wide.

## Treatment

| Decision | Call |
|---|---|
| Photography | **The best ratio on the board.** 29 jobs at 960-1132px, at least one slideshow file at 1920x745, currently shown at 100px. No stock anywhere. Photo-led build, and the before-and-after portico project the About page already describes is the hero candidate. Ask which files are the before and which are the after, because the filenames do not say. |
| Prices | None published. None on the page. Financing is offered and can be named, with no terms attached. |
| Reviews | **Ten, verbatim, with names, and they are readable.** Use Andrew Weise's half-hour-in-the-pouring-rain quote against the 24-hour emergency line, because one proves the other. Print Michael Marr once. |
| Credentials | **Four generations, approximately 100 years, great-grandfather Olin, brothers David and Jack Randall, 20-plus years working together.** All published on their own About page, so all usable exactly as written. Licensed and insured, no number given. Do not turn "approximately 100 years" into a founding year. |
| Structure | The family story is the spine, the way 1939 is for Motor City and 1959 was going to be for Sweers. Names, then the gallery at full size, then the reviews that name Dave and Robert. Add a form and add headings, because there are none. |

**One contradiction to resolve before writing anything.** The homepage advertises an
**emergency line 24 hours a day**. The Schedule of Operation page says
**Monday through Friday 9:00am to 5:00pm, closed Saturday and Sunday.** Ask David
which is true. Andrew Weise's review suggests the emergency line is the true one and
the schedule page is the office, but that is an inference and it does not go on a
page until they say so.

**Do not open by pointing at the 100-pixel thumbnails.** Show the portico at full
width instead. A family that has been doing this since the 1920s will understand
what they are looking at immediately.
