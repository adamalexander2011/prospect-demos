02 / Your project

You don’t need
all the answers yet.

A few details can make the first conversation easier.
Here’s a good place to start.

  1. How will you use it?

    A place to park, work, walk or gather? Think about what you want the space to do.

  2. What’s there now?

    Rough size, existing concrete, access and anything you’ve noticed about water on the site.

  3. What do you have in mind?

    A photograph, a sketch, or just an idea. You can start the conversation without a finished plan.

These are general planning questions, not a quote, technical specification or statement of DeBoer’s process. Scope, suitability and timing need a direct conversation.

About this concept, its sources and what happens next

Carried from published material

  • Business name, geometric logo, historical phone, email and Clio location from DeBoer’s published presence.
  • Seven photographs historically sourced from the business’s public Facebook page. They are not stock or generated images. Public availability does not confirm usage rights or exact job attribution.
  • Greg Taylor’s Facebook recommendation, reproduced verbatim. His project dimensions are not general specifications.
  • The official domain currently shows a Coming Soon page, checked October 7, 2026. It does not currently re-confirm the historical contact details.

Proposed for this design

  • All headlines, buyer-focused body copy, photograph captions, layout and animation are new concept work by Local LVRG, not approved DeBoer statements.
  • The project-note builder is a local demonstration. It collects no contact information, sends nothing, uses no storage and has no CRM, quote or booking connection. Opening an email draft is not sending it.
  • Photographs illustrate possible project conversations, not a verified service list or proof of particular scope. No prices, credentials, years, warranties, deadlines or availability have been invented.
  • This is a separate concept site, not DeBoer’s official website. It is marked noindex and is not an operational customer-acquisition system.

Nothing in the proposed column goes live until DeBoer confirms it. Before launch: confirm contacts, services, service area, photo rights, project attribution and review permission; connect and test the agreed inquiry route. Takedown requests: adam@locallvrg.co.
