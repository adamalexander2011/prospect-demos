# Locke's Landscaping & Brick Paving — Davison, MI

**Status:** read on record, not built
**Their site:** lockeslandscaping.com
**Score:** 30 plumbing + 40 design = **70** (plumbing re-measures at 36 today)
**Read written:** 27 August 2026

## The read

Every image on this website was uploaded in **February 2018**. Every one. Grep the
markup and there is exactly one upload path across every page:
`/wp-content/uploads/2018/02/`. Footer says **© 2018**. Google Analytics is
`UA-114818922-1`, a Universal Analytics property, and Google switched Universal
Analytics off in **July 2023**, so nothing has measured a visitor here in three
years and nobody has noticed.

The trust badge row on the homepage runs Unilock, Angie's List, Houzz, and a glossy
red **Google Plus icon** labelled *"Google Top Rating."* Google Plus was shut down
in **April 2019**.

Now the thing they are sitting on.

`/before-after-landscaping-grand-blanc/` holds **ten matched before-and-after
pairs**. `before1.jpg` through `before10.jpg`, `after1.jpg` through `after10.jpg`.
Same properties, same angles, transformation visible. In landscaping there is no
more persuasive object than that, and there are ten of them.

They are laid out two-up at **653x490** (a couple at 807x605), and the markup says:

```html
<img class="lightbox-false" ... width="653" height="490" src=".../before1.jpg">
```

**`lightbox-false`.** The lightbox is explicitly disabled. 653 pixels is not the
thumbnail, it is the whole thing. And the page is a fourth-level nav item behind
Home, Landscape Services and Our Work.

**The testimonials are the other buried asset**, on their own page, readable in the
DOM, seven of them with names, and every single one names the owner, **Chris
Locke**:

- **Todd T:** *"Chris started when he said he would, finished when he said he would
  and the final cost of the project was exactly what the estimate was."*
- **Janet Q:** *"He broke down the price of his quote (price per sq ft or face foot)
  which made it easy for comparison... He gives a good warranty on his workmanship
  and his installed plants."*
- **Dan T:** *"Chris needed to build a retaining wall 40" high just to get to a
  level grade for the patio."*
- **Lilly O:** *"Chris is on site everyday making sure things are running smoothly."*
- Plus Karen L, Troy R, Ryan J.

A customer volunteering in public that the final invoice equalled the estimate is
the most valuable sentence on this entire board, and it is on page nine.

**The credential is real and verified.** I pulled `unilockcontractor.jpg` and
looked at it. It is the **Unilock Authorized Contractor** mark, 641x234. Worth
noting against Diamond Scape, two towns away, who are running Unilock's product
photography while claiming no relationship at all. Locke's has the actual badge.
`alssaward1.jpg` is an **Angie's List Super Service Award** shield, no year printed
on it.

And one more. Their tenure claim, **"Serving Oakland & Genesee County for over 20
years"**, appears nowhere in the visible copy of the site. It exists only inside
`alt` and `title` attributes on slider images. Twenty years of trading, written
where only a crawler and a screen reader will ever find it.

The tagline **"We Are The 'Everything Outdoors Company'"** is a Slider Revolution
layer, so it rotates past and leaves. The actual headline the probe measured is
34px, 675px down the page, and reads *"Lockes Landscaping, a full-service landscape
design and in..."*, which is the meta description pasted into a heading.

Eleven service pages, five town pages, an Employment page and a "Rate Our Service"
form. Site by Web 7 Marketing Inc.

**Angle:** ten before-and-after pairs of the same yards, buried four clicks deep at 653 pixels with the lightbox switched off.

## Treatment

| Decision | Call |
|---|---|
| Photography | **Ten before/after pairs plus roughly two dozen job photos**, all descriptively named (`landscaping-project-davison-mi-2` through `-9`, `ol6`, `ol9`, `l3`, `l16`, `w2`). All capped at 653-807px, all from Feb 2018. **Ask Chris for the originals and for anything from the last eight years.** If the originals exist, the before/after slider is the whole build, the way it was for Viper. If they do not, 653px still works side by side at moderate size. |
| Prices | None published. None on the page. **Note what Janet Q's review discloses:** he quotes by square foot and face foot. That is his method, published by a customer, not a price. Do not turn it into a number. |
| Reviews | **Use them, verbatim, with the names as given** (first name, last initial, exactly as they appear). Lead with Todd T on the estimate matching the invoice. Seven is plenty. |
| Credentials | **Unilock Authorized Contractor**, verified from the badge image. Angie's List Super Service Award, no year, so state it without one. **"Over 20 years" is theirs and published, in alt text.** It counts, and putting it in visible copy is half the point of the build. |
| Structure | Before/after is the spine, not a page. Then Chris by name, then the reviews that name him, then Unilock. Everything else is a link. |

**Drop the Google Plus badge and do not mention it in the outreach.** It is the
funniest thing on the site and pointing at it makes the first message a joke at
their expense. Same for the 2018 upload dates. Show the before/afters at full width
and the freshness problem argues itself.

**One question decides the size of this build:** has Chris shot any work since
February 2018. Eight years of a Unilock contractor's jobs are missing from the
internet. If he has a phone full of them, this is the best photo-led build on the
board after Motor City.
