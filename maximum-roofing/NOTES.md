skip. `maximumroofingmi.com` scores **40**, well under the 45 line. Fifteen
photographs, six of them 600px or better, the widest at 1920px, and the stock

headline, and 13 third-party hosts. Nobody is going to be wowed by a rebuild of
this, because somebody already did the work.
