## The read

Nick put his email address on his Facebook page. It is
**nick@deboerconcreteandconstructionllc.com**, and it works, because the MX records
point at Google Workspace, which he pays for. He bought the domain through
Squarespace on **21 May 2026**. Three months later, the entire website is four
lines:

> Coming Soon
> deboerconcreteandconstructionllc.com
> We're under construction.
> Please check back for an update soon.

That is the whole page. **3.1KB.** The `<title>` is literally "Coming Soon". The
h1 is the raw domain string. The single image on it is a Squarespace template
default. There is no phone number, no click-to-call, no form, no schema, no
Open Graph, so a link to it shared on Facebook renders as nothing.

He is paying a domain registrar, a Squarespace plan and Google Workspace, and
handing out an address at that domain, and anyone who types the part after the @
gets a holding page.

**Meanwhile the Facebook page is doing the whole job instead.** 301 followers.
A real designed logo, a **DB** monogram over "DEBOER CONCRETE & CONSTRUCTION",
not a font on a photo. At least eight photographs of their own work, and they are
good: interior slabs poured inside wood-framed pole barns, a timber-frame pavilion
with a fresh floor, a covered walkway and drive, an apron at a modern dark-clad
house with a man in a hi-vis vest still working in the frame. No stock, because
none of it is stock.

And a review that can actually be read, which has not been true of a single other
prospect in this repo:

> **Greg Taylor recommends DeBoer Concrete And Construction LLC.**
> *"they did my 30 x 40 barn pad, 6" thick with rebar, absolutely great work! highly recommend."*

Thirty by forty, six inches, rebar. That is the sentence that sells concrete, and
it is sitting on Facebook where it expires off the feed.

**One more, and it is free money.** An exact-phrase local search returns DeBoers in
Grand Rapids, South Dakota and Ontario, and no Clio listing. They are absent from
the Clio concrete map pack entirely. Sitting in it: **Patriot Concrete**, also
Clio, 5.0 from 21 reviews, with a website. **Osborn Inc.**, also Clio, 4.7 from 12,
with a website. Both are being found by people searching for exactly what Nick does
in exactly the town he does it in.

**Angle:** you are already paying for the domain, the hosting and the email. The email works. The website behind it says "under construction."

**Warm opening:** your barn-pad photos are better than anything the guys ranking above you in Clio have on their sites, so I built the site they should have been on. Free, no catch, use it or don't.

**Connection:** family friend. Adam named them directly. Worth noting that they never surfaced in his followed-Pages list, because he does not follow the page.

## Sensitivities

**Do not open with the website.** "Your site says Coming Soon" is a gotcha, and this
is a family friend. Open with the photographs being good, because they are, and let
the finished page make the point by existing.

**Do not lead with the missing Google Business Profile either,** but do mention it
once, plainly, as the free thing it is. It costs him nothing and it is the single
highest-return hour of his week. If he sets it up and asks for nothing else, that
is still a good outcome for Adam.

**He has already decided he wants a website.** He bought the domain, the plan and
the email. That is the strongest buying signal in this repo: nobody has to be
convinced of the premise, he just never got the thing built. Treat it as finishing
something he started, not as fixing something he got wrong.
