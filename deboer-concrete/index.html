Plan the scope

A clear quote starts with a clear scope.

The finished surface is only one part of the decision. Before you compare prices, get clear on the site, the slab and the finish.

01

The site

Access, grade, drainage and what is already there. The first useful conversation starts with the property, not a price list.

02

The slab

Size, intended use, planned thickness, reinforcement and joints. Make the important details explicit before the concrete hides them.

03

The finish

Surface, edges, traction, curing expectations and timing. Get the planned finish and aftercare into the written scope.

Compare what is included, not only the number at the bottom. These are general planning points, not claims about DeBoer’s process or specifications.
